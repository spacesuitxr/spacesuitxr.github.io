Your captured scenes, at room scale, on a standalone Quest.

SpaceSuit View

What it does today

Runs on the headset

Everything happens on the Quest. Your scenes never leave it, and the app has no internet access.

Bring your own scenes

Import PLY files and SuperSplat downloads, converted on the headset in seconds.

Room scale

Grab a scene, resize it, see it whole, or step inside and fly through it.

Measure at real size

Measure between two points and set a known distance to bring the whole scene to real scale.

Early alpha. SpaceSuit View has been tested on one Meta Quest 3 so far. Smaller scenes run at the Quest's full frame rate; larger ones don't yet. See the known limitations.
