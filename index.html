Splat objects and characters, right in your room.

SpaceSuit View

What it does today

Hold it, turn it

Grab a splat with one hand and look at it from any angle, up close, the way you'd handle the real thing.

Any size

Shrink a character to fit on your desk or bring an object to life size, with the thumbstick.

Measure at real size

Measure between two points and set a known distance to bring the splat to its real scale.

Bring your own

Import PLY files and SuperSplat downloads, converted on the headset in seconds. Nothing leaves the Quest.

Early alpha. SpaceSuit View has been tested on one Meta Quest 3 so far. Smaller scenes run at the Quest's full frame rate; larger ones don't yet. Bigger scenes, like rooms, also load, and you can step inside or fly through them, but objects and characters are what it does best today. See the known limitations.
