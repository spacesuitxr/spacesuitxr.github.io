What to expect

About 10 to 15 minutes, seated

What to try

Start a sketch and draw a shape on the board, give it an exact size, extrude it into a solid, then cut a hole into it. If you have time, open one of the sample assemblies.

What helps us most

Where you got stuck in the first few minutes, whether pointing, pinching and grabbing felt reliable, and anything that was hard to read or out of reach.

Controllers or hands

Both work. Hand-tracking feedback is especially welcome.

It's a work in progress

Expect rough edges. Your models stay on your headset; you can export them as STEP or STL.
