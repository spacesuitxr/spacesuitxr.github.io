Privacy

Short version: your models and scenes stay on your headset. Our apps have no analytics, accounts or advertising.

Last updated November 2026. Applies to SpaceSuit CAD (playtest), SpaceSuit View 0.2.0 (alpha) and this website.

The SpaceSuit CAD app

What it asks for

  • Hand tracking, so you can work with your hands as well as the controllers.
  • Spatial anchors and the headset's room data, so a part and the surfaces you mark stay where you put them in your room between sessions.
  • Network access, which the Meta platform components built into the app use. SpaceSuit CAD's own code doesn't send your models, files or room data anywhere.
  • It does not ask for the microphone, the cameras, eye tracking, location or contacts.

What it does with your data

  • Your models stay on the headset. Parts, sketches and assemblies are saved in the app's own storage on the headset. Exports (STEP and STL) are written there too, for you to copy off.
  • Files you import (STEP parts, PDF drawings, images) are opened through the headset's file picker and copied into the app's storage; the app reads only the files you choose.
  • Room anchors and the surfaces you mark are stored on the headset, by the app and by Horizon OS.
  • Head, hand and controller tracking is used frame by frame to draw the scene and handle input. It is not stored or sent by the app.
  • Passthrough is drawn by the headset's system behind the app. The app never receives camera images.
  • Diagnostic logs go to the headset's system log, which is reachable only with developer tools. The app doesn't send them anywhere.
  • No accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting of our own.

The SpaceSuit View app

What it asks for

  • All files access, which you grant in the headset. The app uses it only to list, read, convert and delete scene files in the headset's Download folder.
  • It does not ask for internet access, the microphone, the cameras, hand or eye tracking, location or contacts.

What it does with your data

  • Your scenes stay on the headset. The app reads .ply, .zip and .vsplat files from Download and writes converted scenes there. Importing a zip makes a temporary copy that is deleted when the import ends. It deletes a file only when you confirm.
  • No network. The app has no internet permission, so it can't send or receive anything.
  • Settings (import size and the upright fix) are stored on the headset.
  • Head and controller tracking is used frame by frame to draw the scene and handle input. It is not stored or sent.
  • Passthrough is drawn by the headset's system behind the app. The app never receives camera images.
  • Diagnostic logs go to the headset's system log, which is reachable only with developer tools. The app doesn't send them anywhere.
  • No accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting of our own.

Other apps it opens

Browse SuperSplat and Send feedback open the separate Quest Browser. What the browser and the sites you visit collect falls under their own policies.

Meta's platform

SpaceSuit CAD and SpaceSuit View run on Meta Horizon OS and are built with the Meta XR SDK. What Horizon OS, the Meta Horizon Store and Meta's runtime collect is covered by Meta's privacy policy, not by us, so we don't promise that no data ever leaves your headset.

Playtest invite requests

  • The form on /playtest sends what you type (your name or nickname, email, headset, CAD experience and, if you choose, what you'd design and where you heard about us) to a Google Form. Responses are stored in a Google Sheet that only SpaceSuit XR can open. Google processes it under Google's privacy policy.
  • We use your email only to send your playtest invite and to follow up on the playtest. No newsletters, and we don't share it.
  • We keep requests for the duration of the playtest and delete them within 12 months after it ends. Ask us to delete yours at any time.

Feedback you send us

  • The feedback form on /alpha sends what you type (the report type, headset, app version, scene, size, your description and, if you choose, a way to reach you) to a Google Form. Responses are stored in a Google Sheet that only SpaceSuit XR can open. Google processes it under Google's privacy policy.
  • We use it only to fix and improve SpaceSuit View, and to reply to you if you left contact details.
  • We keep reports for the duration of the alpha and delete them within 12 months after it ends. Ask us to delete yours at any time.
  • If you email us instead, your email stays in our inbox under the same rules.

This website

spacesuitxr.com is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and has no analytics or trackers. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.
